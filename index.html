Freshie Support

Snow forecasts for ski resorts, at the base, middle and top of the mountain.

Contact

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to EMAIL_DO_APP@exemplo.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How do powder alerts work?

Every evening, around 5 PM local time at each resort, we check the forecast for the top of the mountain. If the fresh snow in the next 48 hours reaches the amount you chose (10, 15 or 25 cm), you get a notification. You get at most one alert per storm for each resort.

I’m not getting alerts

  • Check that alerts are on in Profile → Powder alerts, and that the resort is turned on there.
  • Check that notifications for Freshie are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Freshie.
  • Alerts only arrive when the forecast reaches your chosen amount.

I bought Pro, but the app doesn’t show it

Open Profile → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel Freshie Pro?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Freshie. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are the forecasts accurate?

Freshie uses well-known weather models through Open-Meteo, but forecasts in the mountains are hard and can be wrong. Always check the official avalanche bulletin and the resort’s report before you go.
