Freshie shows weather forecasts from computer models. Forecasts can be wrong, especially in the mountains, and conditions can change fast. Snow amounts, scores and alerts are estimates, not guarantees.

Do not use Freshie to make safety decisions. Always check the official avalanche bulletin, the resort’s own report and local authorities before you go, and follow resort rules and closures.
