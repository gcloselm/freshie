Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

Freshie has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email. Most of what you do in the app stays on your iPhone. We only keep a small amount of data on our server when you turn on powder alerts, and you can delete it at any time from the app.

Who we are

Freshie is made by Gabriel Closel, an independent developer based in Brazil, who is responsible for your data. Contact: EMAIL_DO_APP@exemplo.com.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your saved resorts, units and other settings.
  • Copies of recent forecasts, so the app opens fast.
  • Your location, if you allow it. Freshie uses it only to sort resorts by distance, on your device. We never send your location to our server or store it.

Weather forecasts

To show a forecast, the app asks Open-Meteo for the weather at the coordinates of the resort (not your location). Like any website, Open-Meteo receives your IP address when it answers the request. We do not send them anything that identifies you.

Powder alerts (optional)

If you turn on powder alerts, we store the following on our server so we can check the forecast and notify you:

  • A random ID created by the app (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID).
  • Your notification token, which lets Apple deliver notifications to your iPhone.
  • Your alert settings: snow amount, units, and the resorts you want alerts for.
  • Whether you have Freshie Pro, and the dates we sent you an alert (so we don’t repeat it).

Notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service, through the Expo push service. Our server is hosted by Supabase in São Paulo, Brazil.

To delete this data, open Profile → Powder alerts → Delete my alert data. It is removed from our server right away.

Ads (free plan)

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, your IP address and how you interact with ads, to show and measure them. iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you say no, AdMob shows ads that are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Freshie Pro has no ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous ID and your purchase information. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We don’t use analytics or crash-tracking tools.
  • We don’t share data with anyone except the services listed above, which we need to run the app.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time, as guaranteed by Brazil’s LGPD and similar laws where you live. The quickest way is the delete option in the app; you can also write to EMAIL_DO_APP@exemplo.com.

Children

Freshie is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. If the change is important, we’ll also tell you in the app.
